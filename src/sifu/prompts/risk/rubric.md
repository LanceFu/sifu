# Risk rubric

Raise the tier by one for each of: more than three files expected, a public type changes shape, a requirement
marked Implemented is touched, behaviour depends on time or ordering, a rule in the PRD uses the words "never"
or "always".

Go straight to tier 2 for: any protected file, any Constraint requirement (REQ-5, REQ-9, REQ-10, REQ-11),
deleting or migrating stored data, a spec that could be read two ways.

Confidence is how sure you are of the tier, not of the spec. Below 0.5 means choose the higher tier.
